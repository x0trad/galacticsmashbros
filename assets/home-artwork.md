Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: full-screen pixel-art background for Galactic Smash Bros arcade game home screen. Original orbital space station viewed top-down with slight 3/4 perspective, evocative of richly crafted 16-bit adventure game maps. Wide landscape composition, dark navy outer space, distant small stars and a blue planet at upper right, cyan illuminated walkways, lime-lit arena entrances, landing pads with small spacecraft, cargo crates, pipes and mechanical details around the edges. Center is a broad quiet dark teal metal plaza with simple floor tiles, intentionally low detail to support a separate logo and button overlay. Station architecture fills most of image, with space visible beyond its perimeter. Crisp deliberate square pixels, limited palette, strong silhouettes, cyan and lime highlights, subtle violet shadows. No text, no letters, no logos, no interface, no buttons, no watermark. Composition must also crop gracefully to center portrait on phones. Output a polished landscape background asset.
